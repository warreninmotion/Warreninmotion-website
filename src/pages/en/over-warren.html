How it started

Warren in Motion did not start as a plan, but as a search: what makes me happy? The answer became a project that brings together everything I care about.

  • EnergyCreativity, freedom and adventure, and getting the most out of every step.
  • Sport and foodTwo lifelong passions, finally aimed at the same goal.
  • Bigger than myselfUsing it all to help the people who need it most.

That story is in the logo

When the project took shape, it got a face too. The logo tells the same story: from standing still to moving. In five steps, from letters to one continuous line with two wheels.

  1. The lettersW, i and M: the initials of Warren in Motion.
  2. The strokesThe letters reduced to simple lines. Plain and honest.
  3. The wheelsThe wheels bring the letters back: a W and an M appear again.
  4. ItalicSlanted, so the logo wants to move forward.
  5. CompactEverything slides together into one monogram.

Why am I cycling 1,570 km?

Healthy food and exercise should be within reach for everyone, whatever your budget and however busy you are. This ride is the ultimate test of that promise: eight days, seven countries, no shortcuts. And everything I raise goes to the Dutch Food Banks. That way we help people who can't manage it on their own for a while to get healthy food.

Every week you can see how the preparation is going: on the site, on Instagram (@warreninmotion) and on TikTok (@warren.in.motion).
