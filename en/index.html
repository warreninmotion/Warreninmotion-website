15–22 May 2027 · from Lucca to Veenendaal

Cycling 1,500+ km for the food bank

In May 2027 Warren van der Ploeg cycles from Lucca, Italy, home to Veenendaal in eight days. Everything he raises along the way goes to the Dutch Food Banks. Will you ride along?

Why the food bank

In 2025 the Dutch food banks helped 155,600 people, and the number of households grew by 10%. What you give ends up with them.

Who is Warren?

Athlete and foodie with a busy life, juggling work and home like you. He shows how healthy living fits into it, without complicated rules.

Meet Warren

Eight days, seven countries

Along the Ligurian coast, over the Lukmanier Pass and through the Ardennes, all the way home. Around 200 km a stage.
