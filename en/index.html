Lucca → Veenendaal · 15–22 May 2027

Welcome to Warren in Motion

Eight days, seven countries, more than 1,500 kilometres. In May 2027 Warren van der Ploeg cycles from Lucca, Italy, home to Veenendaal, and everything he raises along the way goes to the Dutch Food Banks. Will you ride with us?

The story in short

Who

Warren van der Ploeg

Warren shows that healthy food and exercise simply fit into a busy life. And that this should be within reach for everyone, whatever your budget.

What

Eight days on the bike
