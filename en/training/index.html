Training · on the way to May 2027

How Warren trains

Riding almost 200 km a day on average, eight days in a row, takes solid preparation. Every week you can read here how many kilometres Warren cycled, what kind of rides they were and which heart rate zones he trained in. With a short report from Warren himself.

Heart rate zones

For each ride you can see how much of the time Warren spent in each zone. Zone 2 is the base: easy enough to keep going for hours. Zone 3 and up are the hard efforts.

What you won't see here: on which day or at what time Warren trained, and where he rode. We keep that private. For each week you do see every ride, sorted by length.
