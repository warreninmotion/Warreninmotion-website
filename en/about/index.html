About Warren

Warren van der Ploeg

Healthy food and an active life fit into a busy schedule. Warren shows it every day, and that is what Warren in Motion is all about. The ride from Lucca to Veenendaal is his biggest challenge yet.

How it started

Warren in Motion did not start as a plan, but as a search: what makes me happy? The answer became a project that brings together everything Warren cares about.

Energy

Creativity, freedom and adventure, and enjoying every step of the way.

Sport and food

Two lifelong passions, finally aimed at the same goal.

Bigger than himself

Using it all to help the people who need it most.

Who he is

Warren has been active all his life: strength training, CrossFit, running, hiking, padel and tennis. He has a normal, busy life, with the same rush as everyone else. That is exactly why he shows how healthy living fits in: without complicated rules and without expensive diets.

Why this ride

Healthy food and exercise should be within reach for everyone, whatever your budget and however busy you are. This ride is the ultimate test of that promise: more than 1,500 km in eight days, no shortcuts. And everything Warren raises goes to the Dutch Food Banks.

Follow the preparation

Every week Warren shares how training is going, on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook: @warreninmotion.

The logo

From letters to motion

Five steps from letters to motion. The result: one continuous line with two wheels, the shape of the ride itself.

  1. The lettersW, i and M: the initials of Warren in Motion.
  2. The strokesThe letters reduced to simple lines. Plain and honest.
  3. The wheelsTwo curves and two dots turn it into a bike.
  4. ItalicSlanted, so the logo wants to move forward: motion.
  5. CompactEverything slides together into one monogram, in one continuous line.
