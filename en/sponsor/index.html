For companies

Sponsor as a company

Buy your own stretch of the route, at €20 per kilometre. Your company name rides along on the map, and every euro of your kilometres goes to the Dutch Food Banks. The sooner you join, the further up front you ride.

Calculate your contribution See who is already riding

How it works

Pick your kilometres

From 10 to 100 km, at €20 per kilometre. From 50 km you can also pick a spot for your logo on the jersey.

Ride along, visibly

Your stretch of the route carries your name: in the progress overview and on the follow van, which rides all 1,570 km.

Celebrate with us

On Saturday 22 May we wrap up together with a dinner, the cheque presentation to the Dutch Food Banks and a party. Every sponsorship includes at least one dinner seat (€100).

Calculate your contribution

  1. Pick your kilometres
    €20 per km, from 10 to 100 km. Want more than 100 km? Become main sponsor and we put a package together.
  2. Pick your seats at the table
    Every sponsorship includes at least one seat. Need more? We will sort it out together.
  3. Put your logo on the jersey (from 50 km)
  4. Your contribution
    Total€1.100

Your kilometres go entirely to the food bank. The jersey spots pay for the jerseys and the follow van graphics, and the dinner pays for itself. As a thank-you, every sponsor gets a jersey afterwards. Your invoice comes from the Warren in Motion Foundation (in formation).
