The route

From Lucca to Veenendaal

1,570 km in eight stages, through seven countries. Along the Ligurian coast, over the Alps, through Alsace and the Ardennes, and then home.

Start
Saturday 15 May 2027 · Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, Lucca
Finish
Saturday 22 May 2027 · Veenendaal, expected around 18:00 (to be confirmed)
Follow live

How I ride a stage

  • I ride each stage of around 200 km in blocks of about 50 km.
  • I only stop to eat and for toilet breaks, at the follow van.
  • Between stages I take a necessary sleep block, for safety: riding safely in traffic, staying sharp on the descents and reacting fast when something unexpected happens.
